Privacy Policy

1. Scope & Acceptance
By accessing skilledu.in or enrolling at Skilled.U’s physical institute, you accept this Privacy Policy. If you do not accept it, do not use the website or register at the institute.

2. Minimal Online Collection
We do not collect personal data merely because you browse our website. Skilled.U is not a data-harvesting site. Unless you voluntarily submit information (for example, by contacting us or registering for a course), we collect nothing personally identifiable from website visitors.

3. Data Collected On Enrollment (Physical Institute)
If you join Skilled.U’s physical training programs, we will collect only the minimum required information for administrative and legal purposes, such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Educational or identity documents (if required for certification/compliance)

We do not collect or store unnecessary personal details. Financial transaction records (if any) are processed via third-party payment gateways — we do not retain card details on our servers.

4. Purpose of Use
Data you provide during physical enrollment is used strictly for:

  • Managing registration, attendance, course completion, and certificates
  • Operational communication (schedules, important notices)
  • Legal, tax, and audit compliance where required

5. No Selling or Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties. We will not share your personal data with marketers or data brokers without explicit consent. The only exceptions are: legal obligations, court orders, or government requests.

6. Third-Party Services
We use third-party providers (payment gateways, messaging platforms, video/webinar services). Those services have their own privacy practices. Skilled.U is not responsible for their data handling — review their policies before using them. Examples include payment processors, Zoom/Google Meet, WhatsApp, YouTube, and analytics providers.

7. Cookies & Tracking
We do not engage in invasive tracking. The site may use minimal cookies for basic functionality and anonymous analytics to improve user experience. No profiling or behavioural tracking for advertising is performed by Skilled.U unless explicitly disclosed and consented to.

8. Data Security & Limits
We take reasonable measures to protect information collected for institute operations. However, no system is perfectly secure. Skilled.U is not liable for unauthorized access or breaches caused by factors outside our control (e.g., sophisticated cyberattacks). We will, where applicable, notify affected individuals as required by law or good practice.

9. Retention
We retain student records only as long as necessary for administrative, certification, legal, or tax purposes. If you request deletion, we will remove data unless legal retention obligations or audit needs require otherwise.

10. Your Rights
You may:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion (subject to legal retention obligations);
  • Withdraw consent for communications where consent was relied upon.

11. Children
Our website is not intended for children under 18. If a minor seeks to enroll, parent/guardian consent is required and the parent/guardian must provide information on behalf of the minor.

12. Changes to the Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the website or institute services after such changes constitutes acceptance.
